# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in boxd to be professional, constructive, and safe.
Contributors must communicate with respect, assume good intent where possible,
and focus criticism on code, behavior, and technical decisions rather than
people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing,
deliberate disclosure of private credentials, sustained disruption, and bad
faith participation.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this standard. Report sensitive conduct
issues privately to the repository owner rather than in a public thread.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is not acceptable.
